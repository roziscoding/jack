# Supporting jack

jack is built for fun, in my spare time, and it costs nothing to run — it lives
on your servers, not mine. So there's nothing to pay for here, and I'd rather not
take your money.

If jack has been useful to you and you'd like to give something back, here's what
I'd love instead:

> **Donate to a queer charity or foundation of your choice, in jack's name.**

Pick a cause you care about, give whatever you're comfortable with, and that's it
— that's the whole ask.

## Want to be credited?

Totally optional, but if you'd like a little recognition: open an issue with the
receipt (feel free to redact anything personal — amounts included), and I'll add
you to the supporters list in the README. 💜

Thank you for being here.
